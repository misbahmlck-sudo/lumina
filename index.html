About Us

Meet Dr. Sarah Mitchell

Dr. Sarah Mitchell is a board-certified dentist with over a decade of experience in restorative and cosmetic dentistry. She leads a caring team of specialists who treat every patient like family — listening first, and tailoring each treatment plan to your unique needs.
